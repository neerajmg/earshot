🔊 earshot

Send a file to the device next to you — with sound. No install, no network, no pairing.

Two devices, both on this page: press Listen on the one receiving, then pick a file and press Start sending on the other.

Receive

Press Listen and allow the microphone when the browser asks, then start the sender on the other device. Keep this page in front, the screen awake, and both devices still and close together.


      

        
        

          
          
        

      

      

      

    

  


  

    
Send

    
    

    
Up to 2 MB. Text and documents go faster than photos of the same size.

    
No file handy? The sample is a few lines of text, under ten seconds of sound.

    
Sending from an iPhone or iPad: take it off silent. The side switch
    mutes this kind of sound, and the transfer looks like it is playing either way.

    


    
    

    

    
Sound is a broadcast. Without a passphrase, any device in earshot with this page
    open receives the file. Tell the other person the passphrase some other way.


    

      
      
    

    
    

    
This device keeps playing until you press Stop sending: it
    cannot hear whether the other one has the file, and extra repeats only help. Expect a short
    rising sweep, then two seconds of hiss, over and over. Volume around half — a distorted
    speaker carries less than a quiet one.


    
    
    
A message goes out without a passphrase, so anyone in earshot can read it. Put
    anything private in a file instead.

    


    

      Speed and limits
      

        
file sizehow long

        
      

      
Sound carries about 370 bytes a second, so a short note takes seconds and the
      2 MB maximum takes over an hour. Text usually compresses two or three times on the way out and
      finishes sooner; photos and archives do not. A noisy room needs a few extra repeats, which is
      why the sender keeps playing until you stop it. Anything over 2 MB is refused: at this speed it
      would take hours.
